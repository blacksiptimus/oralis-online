# Oralis

Privacy policy for the Oralis app, published by Aqyrix. The app id is `com.aqyrix.oralis`.

## Delete your data

You can delete your Oralis data without creating an account.

1. Open Oralis, open the guide, and tap Delete. Confirm it. This removes that guide and its recording from the phone.
2. Delete the Oralis app. This removes the remaining guides, recordings, and saved minutes from the phone.

The Oralis server does not keep the recording or the guide after the guide is sent back to the phone. On iPhone, the server keeps an anonymous device key, not your name, so it can check that the app is genuine. To ask Aqyrix to delete that key, email developer@aqyrix.com with the subject “Oralis data deletion”.

Payments are handled by Apple or Google. Deleting the app does not cancel a charge they already made.
