Oralis privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Oralis is published by Aqyrix. This policy explains what the Oralis app for iPhone and Android does with information. The app id is com.aqyrix.oralis.

What Oralis does

You record a conversation. Oralis turns that recording into a guide, such as a recipe, a repair, or a set of steps. You can edit the guide, keep a shopping list, and share it. Oralis does not ask you to create an account.

What stays on your phone

The recording, the transcript, and the guide are saved on your phone. The remaining recording minutes are saved on your phone. Deleting a guide deletes that guide and its recording from the phone. Deleting the app removes these files from the phone.

What is sent to make a guide

When you ask Oralis to make a guide, the app sends the recording, or a transcript you edited, to the Oralis server. The server is in the European Union. The server sends the audio to OpenAI to transcribe it, then sends the transcript to OpenAI to write the guide. The guide and transcript are returned to your phone.

Oralis does not keep the recording or the guide on its server after that response is sent. OpenAI processes the audio and transcript to produce the guide. OpenAI’s handling of API data is described in OpenAI’s privacy policy.

Checking that the request comes from the app

On iPhone, Oralis uses Apple App Attest. The server stores an anonymous device key so it can recognize that phone on the next request. It does not store your name. On Android, the app can send a Google Play Integrity token so the server can check that the install came from Play. These checks are not used to build an advertising profile.

Purchases

The app can be downloaded without paying. Extra recording minutes are bought through Apple or Google. Apple or Google processes the payment. Oralis does not receive your card number. The minutes added to the phone are stored on the phone.

What Oralis does not do

Oralis does not sell your information. The app does not show ads and does not include a third-party advertising or analytics tracker. Oralis does not record a cellular call. A recording starts only when you press record.

Children

Oralis is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
