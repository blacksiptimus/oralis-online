Oralis

Turn a talk into a guide. Published by Aqyrix for iPhone and Android.

Oralis turns a how-to conversation into a guide you can keep. Record two people nearby, record with a call on speaker, or upload a recording you already have. Oralis writes the steps, the things to buy, and the details that matter. You can edit every line. The recording and the guide stay on your phone.

Record

Choose Nearby or On a call, then press record. Nearby is for a conversation in the room. On a call, put the call on speaker and keep the phone close to both voices. Oralis records the room. It does not capture the cellular call itself.

Upload

You can also send a recording you already have. Oralis reads it and makes the same kind of guide.

The guide

Each guide has a title, a short summary, and the steps in order. Important points and warnings are gathered under What matters. Ingredients or parts you need become a shopping list you can check off and add to. You can edit any line, then share the guide as text.

On your phone

Guides and recordings stay on the phone. They are not stored in an account. The first 5 minutes are free. When you need more time, you can buy a minute pack. An account is optional, for keeping minutes when you move to another phone.

Languages

Oralis speaks English, Arabic, German, French, and Spanish. The guide follows the language of the conversation.

Contact

Questions can be sent to developer@aqyrix.com. The privacy policy explains what the app does with information, including how to delete an account.
