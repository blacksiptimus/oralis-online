Oralis support

Help for the Oralis app on iPhone and Android. Published by Aqyrix.

If something in Oralis did not work, or you have a question about a guide, minutes, or your account, write to developer@aqyrix.com. A person at Aqyrix reads that address.

What to include

A short note is enough. These details make the answer faster:

  1. iPhone or Android, and the app version from Settings, About.
  2. What you were doing, such as recording, uploading, buying minutes, or signing in.
  3. What you expected, and what happened instead.

Do not send a recording, a transcript, or a password. Guides and recordings stay on the phone, and a password cannot be reset by email.

Account

You can delete an account in the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. If you cannot open the app, use the steps on the privacy policy.

Contact

Email developer@aqyrix.com. The privacy policy explains what the app does with information.
